# French Atelier · WhatsApp Batch 2 audit

## Current version: functional repair, approved design preserved

This record supersedes the previous restoration and redesign audits. The current delivery is eight new WhatsApp messages under the same eight-topic brief, not copied Batch 1 bodies and not a new website or CRM implementation.

## What is fixed

- Every campaign image is visible; flexbox can no longer collapse the image area.
- Complete bodies and CTA buttons are visible without a clipped phone viewport.
- Mobile cards and metadata fit the viewport.
- The approved CTA labels are now actual, keyboard-accessible hyperlinks to the destinations supplied in Batch 1.
- Original colours, fonts, wordmark, CTA styling and eight metadata fields are retained. Only corrective sizing/wrapping rules were added.
- The new copy and factual/brief corrections are retained, including early school identity, the Quiche sensory sentence, Tour vocabulary integration and Monet image/copy consistency.

## CTA scope, without invented requirements

The original brief requests CTA Button Text and explicitly specifies “Claim it now” for the October and November messages. Batch 1 supplies the school homepage as the destination for all eight, with “See the full story” for the content messages. That approved wording and destination are preserved here.

The current buttons open https://www.frenchatelierlive.com. This is a school homepage, not a verified individual story page or discount-redemption page. The brief did not ask us to create those pages. A working link is not proof of a redeemed discount; no such claim is made.

## Brief checks

- Eight new bodies on Hugo, Amélie, photography, Novembre Numérique, the tricolour, Monet, quiche Lorraine and the Tour de France.
- All bodies remain 150 to 200 words, with separate Header Text and CTA Button fields.
- Three relevant French vocabulary entries in every message; one labelled fun fact per message.
- October photography and November digital-culture offers each give 20% in a separate final paragraph.
- The opening brief says promos 4/8, while detailed instructions and approved Batch 1 specify 3/4; the latter is the documented interpretation.
- No invented teachers, promised created videos, AI Agent, Self Service Area, LMS, scarcity or deadline.
- No em dashes, en dashes or exclamation marks in customer-facing message bodies.
- No ActiveCampaign fields, automations, recipients or schedules changed.

The creative handoff, its links and its rendering are testable here. A live WhatsApp send, provider template approval, complete Sandra-authored ActiveCampaign configuration and discount redemption have not been tested. These must not be confused with the completed copy/image handoff or silently treated as permission to invent new campaign infrastructure.
